The app is how people see and edit a brain without opening GitHub. It renders inside the
conversation in hosts that support [MCP Apps](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/apps/overview),
claude.ai among them, and the same app runs as an ordinary web page for anyone who is not in the
conversation.

<Frame caption="The page viewer: frontmatter as properties, a computed table, and linked references below.">
  <img src="/images/viewer.png" alt="The Isomorphic page viewer" />
</Frame>

Ask Claude to "show me my brain" or to open a page, and it opens the app. The app has no
privileges of its own: everything it shows or changes goes through the same tools Claude uses,
at your role, so it can never do something you could not.

## Opening it in a browser

Every page has a web address, `https://<server>/b/<owner>/<repo>/<path>`, that anyone with access
to the brain can open. They sign in with the same email link and need no Claude account. Ask
Claude to share a page and it gives you the link. Locally, `pnpm try` serves the app at
`http://127.0.0.1:8788/b/local/<folder>`.

## What is in it

- **Viewer.** Rendered markdown with working links in both styles. Frontmatter appears as a
  properties panel. [Computed views](/computed-views) render live, and every page ends with the
  pages that link to it. A refresh control shows the page's age and says when it changed
  underneath you.
- **File tree.** Clicking a folder opens its [folder note](/writing-a-brain#folder-notes). A
  folder without one offers to create it, already holding a listing of the folder.
- **Search**, with ranked results and the lines that matched.
- **Link graph**, with pages sized by how many links touch them and colored by folder.
- **Activity**: who changed what, and when, for the brain or for one page.
- **Brain switcher**, when you can reach more than one brain.
- **Sharing, members and analytics**, each shown only to people whose role can use them.

It follows the host's light or dark theme, respects reduced motion, and can be shown inline,
fullscreen, or picture-in-picture.

## The editor

A rich-text editor that saves the markdown you would have written by hand. That is the whole
design constraint: a brain is read on github.com, in Obsidian and by agents, so an editor that
reformatted every page it touched would make every change unreadable.

<Frame caption="The editor: a formatting toolbar, the page's properties, and the body as rich text, saved back as plain markdown.">
  <img src="/images/editor.png" alt="The Isomorphic editor" />
</Frame>

- **Formatting:** headings, bold, italic, inline code, bulleted, numbered and checklist items,
  quotes, and tables with resizable columns. Undo and redo with the usual shortcuts.
- **Properties** are edited in their own panel and saved with the body.
- **Images:** paste or drop a file. It is stored in the brain beside the page and embedded as a
  normal image link. Saving waits for the upload, so a page never links to a missing file.
- **Faithful saves.** Wikilinks, list markers and tables come back exactly as written, and
  frontmatter the editor does not show is never touched.
- **No lost work.** If someone else changed the page after you opened it, the save is refused
  and your text stays on screen instead of overwriting theirs. On a protected branch, a save
  goes into the brain's open pull request (or opens one), and the editor says so.
